📈 Why Weekly Averaging Matters for Solar Sizing:
Across 1,097 days, your single-day standard deviation is ±11.14 kWh. Over a 7-day rolling window, the standard deviation collapses by 18.1% (to ±9.13 kWh). Days with high cooking, baking, or chores are balanced by lighter days, meaning an array generating 42–45 kWh/day comfortably achieves net-zero electricity across 85%+ of weeks.
