📈 Why Weekly Averaging Matters for Solar Sizing:

Across 1,097 days, your single-day standard deviation is ±11.14 kWh. When evaluating solar coverage over a 7-day rolling window, the standard deviation collapses by 18.1% (to ±9.13 kWh), and over a 30-day billing cycle by 25.4%. Days with high cooking, baking, or chores are balanced by quieter days, meaning an array producing 42–45 kWh/day comfortably achieves net-zero electricity across 85%+ of weeks.
